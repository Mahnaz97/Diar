About Diar ديار

Rooted in Identity, Tailored for Tomorrow

Diar was founded to rescue, elevate, and celebrate the magnificent embroidery traditions of the Hazara people. Hazaragi motifs—complex geometric patterns and vibrant color bursts—are an authentic visual history of resilience and mountain heritage.

By uniting traditional Afghan hand-embroidery with contemporary Western silhouettes for both women and men, we offer accessible luxury for the modern global citizen who values both style and soul.

Hands Behind the Thread

At the heart of Diar is our community of master women artisans working safely and with dignity from their homes across Afghanistan. By providing consistent work, fair compensation, and high-grade materials, we enable these talented women to support their families and secure financial independence.

  • Flexible home-based employment ensuring personal safety and comfort.
  • Direct fair-trade wages supporting household income and children's education.
  • Preservation of ancient Hazaragi needlecraft passed down through generations.
